How It Works

Three steps.
Then just live your life.

1
Pick your funds
Search any mutual fund or ETF by name. Add it to your watchlist in one tap. No account linking, no broker login, no financial data needed.
2
Set your alert rules
Tell us what matters. "Notify me if HDFC Flexi Cap goes up 2%." "Alert me if SBI PSU drops 3%." Simple and yours.
3
We watch. You get notified.
FundPulse checks unit prices every day after market close. The moment your threshold is crossed — you hear about it via Email, WhatsApp, or SMS.
