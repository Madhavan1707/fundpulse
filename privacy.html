Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

FundPulse is a small, independent service that watches the mutual funds and ETFs you choose and notifies you when something happens. This page explains, in plain language, what information we keep and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address — so we can sign you in and send you the alerts you asked for.
  • Your phone number — only if you choose to add one later for WhatsApp alerts. It is optional.
  • The funds you track and your alert settings — that's the whole point of the product.

That's it. FundPulse never asks for — and never wants — your bank details, PAN, Aadhaar, investment amounts, or how many units you hold. You cannot buy or sell anything through FundPulse.

Where it lives

Your account and settings are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Alert emails are delivered through Resend, an email delivery service. Unit price data comes from public AMFI data and news comes from public news sources — neither involves your personal information.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We never send you anything you didn't ask for — alerts only fire on the rules you set.
  • We never contact you on the phone or ask you to invest in anything. If someone does this claiming to be FundPulse, it is a scam.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete account inside the app. This removes your watchlist, alert settings, alert history, profile, and your login itself. There is no "we keep it for 90 days" — it's gone.

Not investment advice

FundPulse sends you information about funds you already chose to follow — price moves, news, and changes. It never tells you what to buy, sell, or hold. Nothing in this app, in our emails, or in our news feed is investment advice or a recommendation. FundPulse is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks — please make your own decisions or talk to a registered adviser.
